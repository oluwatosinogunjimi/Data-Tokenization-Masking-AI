Paste Masker

1. Paste raw text and press Mask. 2. Copy the masked text into your AI chat. 3. Paste the AI's reply at the bottom and press Restore. Tokens stay consistent until you clear the mapping or close the tab. Nothing is saved, and this page cannot make network requests.

Read the masked output before pasting. Pattern matching misses things: names in free text, unusual password formats, IDs only you would recognise. Add anything it missed to Custom terms. Masking reduces risk; it doesn't replace the data-handling rules that apply to you.
